📐 Growth Forecast Model

Our prediction engine uses a simple yet powerful compound growth formula — no ML required.

Growth Rate Formula:
Growth Rate = (Current Price − Previous Price) / Previous Price
Future Price Formula:
Future Price = Current Price × (1 + Growth Rate)^Years
