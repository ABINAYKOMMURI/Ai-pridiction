About This Project

Smart Multi-Category Price Trend Analysis and Future Forecast Dashboard

Objective

To build a comprehensive Data Analytics and Visualization platform that analyzes price trends across 15 product categories using historical and current pricing data, and forecasts future prices using a compound growth model.

  • Track price movements across multiple categories
  • Calculate growth rates from historical data
  • Predict future prices using mathematical growth formula
  • Provide interactive visualizations for data-driven insights
  • Enable custom data upload for extended analysis

Problem Statement

In today's dynamic market, prices of products fluctuate frequently across categories. Consumers and businesses struggle to:

  • Track price trends across diverse product categories
  • Understand which categories show highest growth or decline
  • Predict future prices for budgeting and planning
  • Compare prices across different regions and brands
  • Make informed purchasing and investment decisions

This dashboard solves these problems by providing a centralized analytics platform with forecasting capabilities — without using Machine Learning.

Growth Forecast Model

The project uses a compound growth formula to predict future prices without any Machine Learning:

Step 1 — Growth Rate:
Growth_Rate = (Current_Price − Previous_Price) / Previous_Price
Step 2 — Future Price (3 Years):
Future_Price = Current_Price × (1 + Growth_Rate)³

Example: If Previous Price = ₹1,000 and Current Price = ₹1,100, then Growth Rate = 10%. Future Price = ₹1,100 × (1.10)³ = ₹1,464.10
