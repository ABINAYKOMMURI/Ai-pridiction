Subject Presentation Insights Brief

Presentation Talking Points:
  1. Central Tendency: Contrast the Mean and Median of the pricing columns. Large differences indicate skewed distributions (e.g. outlier prices like vehicles pushing the mean higher).
  2. Dispersion (Standard Deviation): Explain how Gold and Silver have low relative variance (very stable growth rates), while Mobiles and Laptops display a wide Standard Deviation due to rapid depreciation cycles.
  3. Model Transparency: Highlight that the model operates deterministically using pure algebraic ratios ($Years=1$), ensuring that it can be audited by hand for exam assessments.
